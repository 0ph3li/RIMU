01 — pick a bird

are waiting for you.

Real names, given by the rangers. Each one can be adopted by forty people at most, so the letters stay personal. Click one and it's yours on the card below.

02 — sign the card

Write your name.
The card does the rest.

This is the first postcard you get. It goes on the hut wall on the island too, next to the others. Download it if you like.
